Common Questions

FAQ

How long does a made-to-order piece take?

Turnaround depends on the garment and current order volume. We'll confirm a timeline when you book your fitting.

Do I need to come in for a fitting?

For made-to-order pieces, yes — accurate measurements are how we guarantee fit. Ready-to-wear pieces in the shop don't require a fitting.

Can I supply my own fabric?

Yes. Let us know during booking, or mention it when you reach out. We can also source fabric for you.

Do you handle bulk or corporate orders?

Yes — churches, event entourages, and offices. See our Corporate page for details.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment details are confirmed during booking or checkout.

Do you deliver outside Ekpoma?

Yes, delivery is available. Let us know your location when booking or reaching out.
