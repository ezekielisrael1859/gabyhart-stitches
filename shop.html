Size Guide

Find Your Fit

Not sure how to measure yourself? Use a soft tape measure, stand naturally, and have someone help if possible. You can send us these numbers when you book a fitting — or skip this and we'll measure you in person.

Chest

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms, keeping it level across your back.

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, where your torso bends when you lean side to side.

Hip

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your hips, keeping it level.

Shoulder Width

Measure from the edge of one shoulder to the other, across your back.

Sleeve Length

With your arm slightly bent, measure from the shoulder edge to your wrist bone.

Neck

Wrap the tape around the base of your neck, where a shirt collar would sit.

Trouser Length

Measure from your waist down to where you'd like the trouser hem to fall.

Inseam

Measure from your crotch down to your ankle, along the inside of your leg.
