Fabric & Sourcing

A piece is only as good as what it's cut from. Here's what goes into every order.

Where It Comes From

Sourced with the piece in mind

Fabric is chosen for what the garment needs to do — breathable cotton for everyday senators, structured weaves for agbada, denim weight that holds shape without stiffness.

We work with trusted local suppliers first, and source further out when a client's request calls for something specific. Every fabric is checked by hand before it's cut.

What We Work With

Fabric on Offer

Cotton

Breathable, everyday-friendly. Used across senators, shirts, and casual wear.

Brocade & Aso Oke

Structured, occasion-ready. Common in agbada and wedding pieces.

Denim

Mid-to-heavyweight, holds its shape while staying comfortable to move in.

Linen Blends

Lightweight and breathable — a common request for warm-weather kaftans.

Have your own fabric?

We're happy to work with fabric you already own or source yourself. Bring it in, or mention it when you book a fitting.
