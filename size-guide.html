Before You Book

Size Guide

Six measurements, a soft tape, and a few minutes. Have someone help you if you can — it's more accurate than measuring alone.

What You'll Need

  • A soft measuring tape (not a rigid ruler)
  • A well-fitted shirt or light clothing — not bulky layers
  • A second person to help, if possible
01

Chest

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms and across your shoulder blades. Keep the tape level and snug, not tight.

02

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline — usually just above your belly button, at the narrowest point. Don't suck in; stand naturally.

03

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, roughly 8 inches below your waistline. Keep the tape parallel to the floor.

04

Shoulder

Measure across your back from the edge of one shoulder to the edge of the other, where the shoulder meets the arm.

05

Sleeve Length

With your arm slightly bent, measure from the edge of your shoulder down to your wrist bone.

06

Height

Stand straight against a wall, without shoes. Measure from the floor to the top of your head.

Not sure about your numbers?

You can skip exact measurements when booking and mention it in the notes — we'll follow up before cutting any fabric.
