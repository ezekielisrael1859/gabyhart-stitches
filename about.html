Materials

Fabric & Sourcing

A good garment starts before the first cut. Fabric choice determines how a piece drapes, wears, and holds up over years, not just weeks — so it's treated as seriously as the stitching itself.

Every fabric used at Gabyhart Stitches is selected for weight, weave, and finish appropriate to the piece — structured cottons for senators, breathable blends for everyday shirts, heavier denim for street wear built to last.

Weight & Drape

Fabric selected to match how a piece is meant to move and sit.
