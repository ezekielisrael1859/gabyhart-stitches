Our Story

About GABYHART STITCHES

Five years of tailoring in Ekpoma. Built on one rule: pay attention to details.

The Founder

Gabriel Osaretin Aigbedion

GABYHART STITCHES started in Ekpoma five years ago with a simple standard: every piece leaves the workshop fitting the way it was measured to fit. No shortcuts on stitching, no guessing on sizing.

What began with senators and agbada for individual clients has grown into full outfitting for weddings, churches, offices, and events — ready-to-wear for those who need it fast, made-to-order for those who need it exact.

The name carries the standard forward: GABYHART STITCHES. Pay attention to details.

Measured, not guessed

Every made-to-order piece starts with real measurements, not a size chart.

Built for the occasion

Weddings, corporate, everyday — each piece cut for what it's actually worn for.

Finished with intent

DTF monogramming, fabric sourcing, and stitching all checked before delivery.
