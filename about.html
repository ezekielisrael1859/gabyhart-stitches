About Us

Our Story

Certified Obioma

GABYHART STITCHES started in Port Harcourt in 2020 as Hart Stitches. In 2021, the brand moved to Ekpoma, Edo State, where it continued to grow through quality craftsmanship, consistency, and attention to detail.

In 2024, Hart Stitches was rebranded as GABYHART STITCHES, reflecting the brand's growth while staying true to its roots.

Today, GABYHART STITCHES creates quality menswear, including kaftans, native wear, corporate outfits, streetwear, and custom-made pieces. Every garment is made with care, precision, and a commitment to excellence.
